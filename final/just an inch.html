Just An Inch is a leave-behind booklet I used to explore themes of girlhood, rebellion, representation, and diversity in haircare through playful illustration. The purpose of the project was to create a small leave-behind that shows your skill as a designer. This 5 x 5 in, staddle- stitched booklet was meant to serve as a brief introduction to my illustrative and design skills, while exploring themes I was currently interested in. The booklet goes through a variety of hairstyles on diverse ‘characters’, boldy presentating their hairstyles.
