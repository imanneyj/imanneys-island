My Black Hair Cyanotype Scarfs serve as a machine that translates black motifs and artifacts associated with black hair culture onto cotton headscarves through sunlight. Scarves in themselves represent a large part of what Black hair culture personally means to me, as someone who is both culturally African and African American through two different sides. These scarves represent a bridge between African and Black American culture that is equally evident though the objects I have chosen as my inputs/source material. I mainly utilized design visuals on African braiding charts like bursts and numbered braiding styles. I also use the symbol of the Black Power Pick, which spoke to how Black empowerment can be expressed through hair care. These cotton scarves and negative process film designs are 22 x 22 in.
