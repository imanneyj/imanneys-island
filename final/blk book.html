The Black Book is a handhand book that explores of how I define black culture through the lense of black photography, specfically photography capturing predominately black spaces and communities. As someone who is both African American and Ivorian, my idea of black culture spans across the world. The Black Book pulls from cultures througout the whole black diaspora, including Jamaican, African, American and British photography. This is the first and longest book I’ve handbound. I used this book to explore photo treatment, color and layout. The book is 5.5 x 9.5 in, 500 pages, laser-printed and perfect bound.
